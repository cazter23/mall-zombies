# Sunset Galleria: what each test file covers

`tests/bugHunt.test.js` covers the November 2026 bug hunt, one regression test per bug, each in the real headless game (the list with what the player saw: docs/history.md). Ammo and items: the Full Refund label's "round back" never lowers a reserve that is above its cap; a gun whose config reserve is 0 resolves to 0 (no phantom Leaf Blower round, so a Platinum one runs dry and claims); a Stuffing that left today's bins while the order form was open cannot be ordered. Saves: Employee Discount's coupon is not kept by a save (prices are full after Continue); Save & quit right after Stay for overtime holds the win; a Second Chance already spent is not announced again on Continue; a claw machine saved during its site's last grab comes back sold out and restocks with the round. Pay and credit: Store-Wide Clearance never kills a boss, RUNG UP included; Build-A-Blaster counts a projectile's kill for the gun that threw it, not the gun in hand. Guns: a tennis ball thrown with your face against a wall stays on your side; a holstered gun's fire delay runs out and a Leaf Blower's tank refills on your hip. The horde: in each known pocket (Food Court bench pocket and south-west nook, the parking kerb island) spawn markers keep a route, the round can spawn and a zombie comes closer; every body-standable spot on every ramp is on its nav link; zombies a floor apart do not shove each other; the failsafe rescues a zombie wedged on the floor above you; a boss's backup announces its type with the zombie and marks the guide page seen. The map: no body fits on the known perches (TV wall, arcade cabinets, a gondola, the escalator's side walls, two store counters); a sprint-jump up an escalator never puts the feet under the treads. Waste: the HUD hears about the boss voucher only when it changes; a Sentry with nobody in sight looks again a few times a second; a menu opened mid-jump lets the fall finish and still holds you on the ground. The decisions taken afterwards: a boss's reward on the floor or still to fall at the round's start is saved and falls again on load; a claw grab the save dropped is refunded (and a sold-out machine from an older save stays closed); Second Chance keeps Bubble Wrap; a reissued Return to Sender has the ammo the last one had, also over a save; a Flash Sale group is eligible only if it marks a buy spot; a fleeing Shoplifter that is staggered stops and its getaway clock waits; the parking kerb island and the slot beside the first van hold no body; a newer build's save is left in its slot and the slot's notice is handed out once; blocked storage and a refused write grey Save & quit out with the reason; two career-stats ledgers on one store add up. A Shoplifter that gets away once the run is over says nothing on the PA (the event still fires). (The receipt's key guard is in tests/controllerMenus.test.js.)
