# Sunset Galleria: folder structure (every module)

One entry per module, under its folder: what it offers (its interface) and what a reader of OTHER modules must know about it.
How a module works inside is in its own header comment. Grep for the file name. tests/docs.test.js checks that every file in src/
has an entry here. How the systems fit together: "How the pieces connect" at the end.

```
index.html              Entry page: just src/boot.js and the tab icon (src/icons/icon-64.png). The web fonts are bundled (src/fonts/), never linked.
package.json            The npm commands (what each does: CLAUDE.md Commands; tools/ below names the script behind each). Dependencies: three, and vite (dev).
vite.config.js          Dev server only: compiles the game at server start (warmup) and skips .claude, dist, dist-single, references and scratch. Paths are
                        absolute from this checkout, so a worktree keeps its own hot reload.
play.cmd                Double-click version of `npm run play` (tools/play.mjs).
src/                    The game. Everything the browser loads starts at boot.js.
  boot.js               The page's entry point: waits for the web fonts (core/fontsReady.js; list and time limit in config/fonts.js), then imports main.js.
  main.js               BROWSER SIDE ONLY: renderer, scene, camera, input + gamepad, HUD, sound, viewmodel, player body, menus and the controller menus
                        (handleControllerMenus), pointer lock, the debug keys (handleDebugKeys, run each live tick through createGame's debugTick), look input
                        (applyLookInput), render(alpha) + the death camera. In debug mode it exposes window.__mall: step(n), game (= g.state), g,
                        movedWhileFrozen().
  style.css             HUD basics (crosshair, hit marker, health/stamina bars, ammo, prompt), start/pause overlay. The retail look on top: ui/retail.css.
  icons/                icon-64.png = the tab icon (index.html; tools/buildSingle.mjs inlines it as a data: URL). sunset-galleria.ico = the same for a Windows
                        shortcut (nothing in the build reads it).
  fonts/                The web fonts, bundled so the game and the single-file build show them offline (no font host is ever contacted).
    fonts.css           GENERATED by tools/buildFontCss.mjs from config/typefaces.js: one @font-face per bundled face (Latin subsets); boot.js imports it.
    files/*.woff2       The Latin-subset font files (Fontsource builds of Google Fonts families, all SIL OFL or Apache 2.0).
    LICENSES.md         The font credits: each family's licence and copyright notice, with a link to its full text.
    licenses/*.txt      Each family's full licence text with its copyright notice, copied unchanged from its @fontsource package.
  config/               EVERY tunable number (CLAUDE.md rule). Systems read these through the stats object or g.pricing where those exist.
    arenas.js           ARENA SURVIVAL (Extra Modes; docs/archive/arena-modes-plan.md): the three arenas in menu order (name, menu line, round 1 PA, zones,
                        player spawn, extra spawn markers, arena-only fixtures: RESTOCK-O-MATIC, Customer Service desk or Box Office windows, wall guns on fence
                        boards, the WEAR-O-MATIC), plus the menu, HUD, receipt and record words. arenaById, ARENA_IDS.
    atrium.js           The Grand Atrium's authored dressing (atriumDetails: shops, kiosks, murals, fountain, overhead pieces); `at` = metres along a wall from
                        the room's min X/Z, so moving the room moves its dressing. Its shops are scenery behind sealed glazing.
    attachmentVisuals.js Per-weapon attachment sizing, near-side rail placement, short-barrel spans and held reload copies.
    audio.js            Master volume and chain (compressor, `limiter`, `softClip`), `voices` caps, `reverb` + `rooms` (presets by kind of room) + `roomOf`

                        send, maxVoices, duck...). Merges config/gunSounds.js over its sounds.
    bosses.js           The bosses: debut windows (none for the booked-only Last Customer), parking markers, wave timing (bossTiming, encores), combat and
                        control tuning, bossComfort, PA lines (introBanner, encoreBanner pools, phases, defeatBanner), bossSpectacle, the Janitor's Wet Floor,
                        the Last Customer's `cart`. Kill values and health: economy.js.
    careerStats.js      Career schema versions, recent-run limit and accuracy record threshold.
    challengeBoard.js   The Challenges tab and the challenge tracker: maxPins (3), the pins' storage key/version, the board's words, slot tags, the tracker's
                        seconds (shown, struck through), words and soft fail sound.
    cinema.js           Sunset Cinema (upstairs wing): lobby, Screen 1, passage, seat blocks, sealed connections, spawn markers, colours, two pooled lamps,
                        spatial ambience and slide data. Absolute coordinates; the door price is economy.doors.cinema.
    cinemaDetails.js    The cinema's authored cosmetic dressing (maps/mall/cinemaDetails.js and its sub-passes). Never collision, spawns or tuning.
    cinemaFinish.js     The cinema's second art pass: service runs, equipment landmarks, lobby entrances, carpet bounds and fabric bays
                        (maps/mall/cinemaPublicFinish.js, cinemaServiceFinish.js).
    cinemaStairs.js     The cinema's west exit -> service stairwell -> Service Corridor north end: bounds, ramp plateaus, tread count, lower door opening,
                        painted light. Absolute heights; the flight is a ramp + nav link.
    clawMachine.js      GRAB-A-GUN: prize weights and capsule colours, the sweep (time, speed-up a grab, floor), auto-drop, lift and take times, stock a site
                        and plush odds, the cabinet's size, its six sites, the PA lines, the show. Price: economy.claw (g.pricing 'claw').
    closingMusic.js     "Last Call", the Closing Time round's song (data for audio/mallMusic.js, `chime: false`, `loop: true`), its volume, fades, the radio
                        duck and the resolving chord. audio/sound.js playScore / stopScore play it; main.js decides when.
    closingTime.js      Closing Time: the four duties and their keys and places, the targets, PA copy (`keep` lines are never dropped), the receipt's words and
                        art, `overtime`, Statistics labels, key and prop tuning, the escape van (`vanShow`: fx/vanLights.js) and the validation machine, the run

    closingTimeSim.js   Developer-only measurement policy for tools/closingTimeSim.mjs (seed/worker limits, skill presets, quest routing, timeouts). The game

                        the one style for new direction signs.
    controls.js         bindings (action -> keys/mouse + Pad* codes; menu* = the in-run menus; ui* = the menus around a run, CONTROLLER ONLY, since a key there
                        would press a page button twice), menuNav (repeat, thresholds, scroll), menuHints, buttonLabels, controlsHelp (the Controls screen; keep
                        in sync with bindings), gamepad (deadzones, look curve, turn ramp, triggers, click-to-sprint), aimAssist.
    deathMusic.js       End of Shift: the seven-second death composition (piano/pad/bell/noise layers), gain and stop fade. The bell anchors to
                        feel.death.resultsDelay.
    deathReports.js     Satirical OSHA / Mall HR incident reports and survival tips per lethal source (Form 109-C). health.takeDamage(amount, source) records
                        the killing blow: { type: 'zombie', id, variant?, boss? }, { type: 'fall' } or { type: 'hazard', id } (a hazard that hurts the player

    debug.js            DEBUG MODE (`mode`: the dev server, ?debug or the saved Settings > Debug switch; game/debugMode.js) and what it turns on (keys,

    decor.js            Decoration kit + clutter scatter numbers (world/decor.js, world/scatter.js). Cosmetic only.

    economy.js          Points, payouts, kill values, door prices keyed by DESTINATION area (economy.doors), wall buys, ammoCache, upgrades, wardrobe,
                        wearOMatic, kiosk, claw, hazards, secondChance, closing, flashSales, rounds (incl. clockIn), waves, and the helpers

    environment.js      Cosmetic lighting/materials (hemisphere + fill sky lights), ceiling spacing, subtle local motion, contact shadows, `atmosphere.ambience`

    feel.js             How the player moves and the view feels: movement, stances, vault (+ ledge), fall damage, look and recoil spring, ADS, camera, player
                        size, sim tick rate, shutter speed, the viewmodel handling every gun shares, hit effects, crosshair, body, health (regen, hurt edges,
                        secondChance), death camera, sprintFx, weaponSwitch, interact (hold time, range, facing), stragglers, world.wallBuyDisplay, the melee
                        lunge.
    fonts.js            The startup font wait, derived from config/typefaces.js families (weights asked for, time limit).
    guide.js            THE STAFF SURVIVAL GUIDE: every page (`guideEntries`: subject, health, first round, `notesAt` / `tipAt` kills, memo, notes, tip),
                        incentiveEntries (from config/powerUps.js), guideEntryId(type), guideStage(entry, record), guideStorage, the PA line, the screen's words

    gunPalettes.js      Shared role bindings for each model colour key, and model colour defaults.
    gunSounds.js        Every gun's sounds (fire as crack / body / mech parts, dry fire, each reload step, impacts), the box cutter, the Sentry, the hit

    gunStyle.js         How upgrades change a gun's SHAPE and colour, looks only: attachment scale (weapons/models/upgradeParts.js), the warranty collar and tag
                        sizes and METAL finish per tier (warrantyTrim.js), the build's pillows, quality vividness and glow, Heart badges, the seam and `rim`

    haptics.js          Controller vibration (core/haptics.js): intensity, per-gun/damage/pickup durations, motor strengths and priorities; moments (blast,

    radioTracks.js      Galleria FM's six original songs as data (After Closing, Escalator Bossa, Food Court Funk, Synth Promenade, Waiting On Hold, Clearance

                        game/secondChance.js calls it inside onDeath. `invulnerable` = god mode.
    stats.js            createPlayerStats(): THE single stats object (health, damage taken, speeds incl. surfaceSpeed, stances, fall and blast damage, vault,

                        multipliers, instantKill, bossDamageMultiplier; armourDefaults for Bubble Wrap). resetPlayerStats(stats) restores every stat (same

    vault.js            The low-obstacle vault and the balcony-rail climb (feel.vault): it goes where you LOOK, only over an obstacle you face; a held jump (the
                        magnet) only vaults the obstacle it was pressed at (ahead(), tryStart({ magnet }), preview() for the prompt). Path, headroom and landing

                        interrupts. Balcony rails (collider.ledge) are climbed straight over, then a drop with no steering (chooseDrop picks a clear landing).

    wardrobeAppearance.js Browser side: equip and reset wiring for the Wardrobe's look on the body and the first-person arms (config/wardrobe.js wardrobeLook).
```

## How the pieces connect
- Loop: `main.js` owns it. Every frame starts with `applyLookInput(frameTime)` (a fresh gamepad poll, mouse + stick look, aim-assist
  slowdown) BEFORE the ticks, so this frame's shots and movement use where you are looking now. Then the fixed 60 Hz ticks (each:
  `gamepad.poll()`, `handleControllerMenus()`, `g.update(dt)`), then `render(alpha, frameTime)`.
- `g.update(dt)` (game/createGame.js), in order: the collider grid check, ambience and the sound stage; if dead, only the death timer,
  zombies and health (the mouse is released on death, so this is checked before the pause check); if not running, the player freezes;
  otherwise the debug keys (main's handleDebugKeys through the debugTick hook), pricing, `player.update` (standing still behind an
  in-game menu), power-ups, Closing Time, the Return, `interactions.update` (before the gun, so a controller-X hold near a buyable
  buys instead of reloading), melee, equipment, `gun.update` (blocked by a swing, a throw or a vault), warranty, `zombies.update`, the
  balls, rockets and thrown things (after the zombies moved), Mascot costumes, then (only in the mall, not the test range) bosses,
  clock-in, `rounds.update` and stragglers; health, `map.update`, door openings and their sounds, `features.update`, bag drops, the
  outfit rail, challenges, Flash Sales, the test range, `player.updateView` (recoil springs last, so a shot's kick shows on the next
  frame), `saveGame.update()` (a round that began this tick is snapshotted now) and `input.endTick()`. `rounds.start()` happens the
  first tick the game actually runs.
- Running: `state.running` = alive, not waiting at the van (`winPending`), and the pointer locked OR `state.padPlaying` (started or
  resumed with a controller: A/Start, no pointer lock needed) OR `forceRun`. Esc (or Start) pauses and shows the pause screen;
  unplugging the controller mid-game pauses. An in-game menu a feature opens (`state.menu`, e.g. the Build-A-Blaster order form)
  stops the player (no moving, shooting or buying) but not the world; the feature reads menu actions (config/controls.js menu*) in
  features.update. While Settings or Controls is open, `handleControllerMenus()` hands controller input to its padNav.
- Buying: createGame adds every `map.doors` entry to `interactions` (verb 'unlock', cost `pricing.priceOf('door', destinationAreaId)`,
  use = `map.areaUnlocks.unlock(destinationAreaId)`, which owns only that destination and opens every barrier between owned areas;
  zombies start spawning there and the nav grid rebuilds as each shutter's collider goes) and builds every `map.features` entry by
  kind (game/features/). Every price comes from g.pricing.
- Map: createGame does `loadMap(mapId, scene, colliders)` and only uses the map interface (maps/index.js). The zombie manager gets
  `map.spawnPoints` + `isZoneOpen`, so zombies only spawn in opened zones.
- Zombie types: rounds picks each spawn's type (economy.zombieMix) and calls `zombies.spawn(typeId)`; health is
  `zombieHealthFor(typeId, round)`, kill value `economy.killValues[typeId]`. A type's first spawn in a run -> 'typeIntro' -> the PA
  shows its `type.introBanner`. Zombie sounds: zombie -> ctx.onSound -> manager (dedupe) -> game/soundStage.js -> a positional voice.
- Points: gun and melee call `onHit({ target, killed, headshot, melee })` -> createGame emits 'hit' (a non-lethal zombie hit: +10); a
  zombie dying (zombie.takeHit records `killedBy`; the manager's onKill) -> 'kill' -> kill value + head / melee bonus (`pointsForHit`)
  and run stats, whatever made the kill. Nothing is paid or counted once the player is dead; a hit on a target already dead is skipped.
- Death: `onPlayerDeath()` (createGame.js) sets `state.over` and hides the combat HUD; main's hook releases the mouse. Render runs the
  death camera (drop + roll) and skips the gun pass; after `feel.death.resultsDelay` the results screen shows. Play again ->
  `resetRun()` (createGame's resetSteps) -> re-lock.
- Testing hook: `window.__mall.step(n)` advances the real loop n ticks and draws a frame (use with `game.forceRun = true`; set it back
  to false before screenshots, or the live loop keeps playing); `step` applies pending look first. Tests use tests/helpers/headlessGame.js.
- Render: the world pass, then (after `clearDepth`) the viewmodel scene (`renderer.autoClear` is false). Paused, it uses alpha = 1
  and zero visual time, so nothing twitches; after death visual time keeps flowing. Settings (game/settings.js) write straight into the
  config objects, so whatever reads config each frame picks them up live.
- Shooting: `player.getAim()` gives eye + direction including view kick; `aim.applySpread()` then randomizes it inside the spread
  cone. The crosshair gap is computed in `main.js` render from `aim.spreadDegrees` and the camera FOV (gap px = tan(spread) /
  tan(fov/2) * screenHeight/2), so every shot lands inside the lines. Camera pitch kick is part of the aim (and springs back to exactly
  where you aimed); camera roll, gun kick, sway, bob and the muzzle flash are visual only (shots never come from the gun model).
  Audio starts on the first click or key (`sound.unlock()`).
- Collision: one shared flat array `colliders` of `THREE.Box3` (created in createGame, filled by the map and the test range) +
  `colliders.ramps`. `kit.box(..., { collide })` makes a mesh and optionally its collider (some visuals use `collide: false` with a
  hand-sized `addCollider`). The player is a cylinder. `ignoreShots` boxes block walking, not bullets; `ledge` boxes are balcony rails.
  g.shootables holds the test-range dummies; zombies are hit through the zombie manager.
- Coordinates: metres, +Y up; the camera looks down -Z at yaw 0.
- Markers (invisible; `debug.showMarkers` shows them): `map.spawnPoints`, each tagged with its zone and its floor height (per area:
  docs/map.md); wall buys are 'wallBuy' features with an outward `normal` + `weapon` id, priced through g.pricing.

## The Staff Training Center (maps/testRange/, debug mode only)
- A 60 x 60 m hall centred at (0, -100), far south of the mall: block walls to a 9.5 m ceiling (clear of your eyes on the 6.3 m
  balcony), fluorescent strips, safety stripes, staff signage (backOfHouse.js). One lamp of 50 m reach plus the mall's two sky lights
  (config/environment.js), so the mall looks the same with or without it. T teleports there and back.
- Main hall: a runway with a line every 5 m, slide bars and an 8 m low tunnel (underside 1.2 m: crouch fits, standing doesn't),
  pillars, jump boxes 0.3 to 1.2 m (1.2 deliberately unreachable), sprint-jump platforms with 2 to 5 m gaps, five practice dummies, and
  the movement course (movementCourse.js: the vault row, the ramp to a 3 m landing and the 6.3 m balcony, the reach rule) with the
  movement readout.
- Through the north wall THE SHOOTING GALLERY (gallery.js; targets at config/debug.js gallery.distances, the counter steps their
  round); right of the spawn THE ARMORY BENCH (armory.js); west THE ZOMBIE PEN (pen.js, its console just inside the doorway).
